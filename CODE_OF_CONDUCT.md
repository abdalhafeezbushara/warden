# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Warden to be a welcoming, harassment-free project for everyone,
regardless of experience, background, or identity.

## Standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer
via the contact in the repository. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
